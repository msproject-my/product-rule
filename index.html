Complete Guide to the Dynamic Product Rule

1. When is the Product Rule Used?

The Product Rule is used when a function is composed of the product of two algebraic expressions containing the variable x, expressed in the form y = u · v.

2. What are the 5 Main Learning Steps?

  1. Identify u & v: u is the front function and v is the back function.
  2. Differentiate: Find derivatives du/dx and dv/dx using the chain/power rule.
  3. Substitute into formula: The formula is dy/dx = v(du/dx) + u(dv/dx).
  4. Factorize: Factor out the highest common factors (lowest power brackets & coefficients).
  5. Simplify: Expand remaining terms inside brackets and combine like terms.

3. How to Use This Simulator

  • Select the question structure on the left panel first.
  • Customize the expression and power values for your specific question.
  • Use the quick power toolbar buttons (x², x³) or type normally without worrying about the ^ symbol.
  • Click "Simulate & Calculate Steps" to view the full step-by-step solution.
